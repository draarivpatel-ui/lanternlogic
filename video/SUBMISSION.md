# Lantern Logic | Pumpkin Playground

Name: Lantern Logic, a pumpkin carving puzzle game

Description:
Lantern Logic is a puzzle game about carving the right pumpkin for every trick or treater. Each visitor arrives with shape hints: triangle eyes, a round mouth, maybe a demand for symmetry. Carve on a grid pumpkin with arrow keys and space or with touch, and a deterministic engine scores shape match, symmetry, and leftover shell waste, then delivers a verdict with a wink. Twelve hand designed levels escalate the constraints: mirror symmetry, a protected stem zone, cut budgets, and finally all three at once. The night sky, moon, costumed silhouettes, and your flickering lantern are all drawn in code with zero image assets and zero dependencies. Fully keyboard operable with a live region narrating moves, and your progress saves locally. Forty one unit tests cover the carving engine, level integrity, and scoring.
