Welcome to Lantern Logic, a puzzle game about carving the right pumpkin for every trick or treater. Each visitor arrives with shape hints. Triangle eyes, a round mouth, maybe a demand for symmetry. You carve on a grid pumpkin with arrow keys and space, or with touch. The engine scores your carve on shape match, symmetry, and leftover shell waste, then delivers a verdict with a wink. Twelve hand designed levels raise the stakes. One demands mirror symmetry. Another protects the stem zone. Later levels cap your cuts and combine every rule at once. Stars twinkle, the moon glows, and your lantern flickers as it takes shape. Everything you see is drawn in code. No images, no engines, no dependencies. A pure engine, deterministic levels, and a score you can replay.
